How Remote Power Works

Power OFF vs Power ON over the Internet

1. Turning OFF your PC from your Phone

As long as your PC is running, Cloudflare Tunnel securely bridges your phone directly to Windows. Clicking "SHUT DOWN PC" triggers shutdown /s /t 10 and your PC cleanly powers off.

2. Turning ON your PC when it is completely OFF

When your PC is powered off (0W power), Windows and Node.js are stopped, so this PC cannot receive internet commands. To turn it on remotely, choose either of these two standard methods:

Method A: Smart Plug ($8) + BIOS AC Restore (Easiest)

Plug your PC into a Smart Plug (Tapo / Tuya / Kasa). In your PC BIOS, set "Restore AC Power Loss" to Power On. Whenever you want to turn on your PC from outside, toggle the Smart Plug in your phone app!

Method B: Always-On Device on Home Wi-Fi

Run SWITCH on an old Android phone, Raspberry Pi, or home server on your home Wi-Fi. That device stays online 24/7 and broadcasts the Wake-on-LAN packet to your PC's MAC address.

Why Won't the PC Turn On? WoL Guide

Troubleshooting Wake-on-LAN for RHU IT Gaming PC

Wake-on-LAN requires standby power and network routing. Check these 4 critical reasons why a PC doesn't wake up:

1 Check Physical Ethernet Light (Most Important!)

When this PC is turned off, look at the back where the Ethernet network cable plugs in:

🟢 LED light is ON or BLINKING: Network chip is powered and ready!
🔴 LED light is COMPLETELY OFF: Motherboard has cut power. It will NEVER wake until fixed in BIOS/Windows (see below).
2 Router Network Barrier (Different Subnet) Common Issue

Your Gaming PC is on 192.168.68.62 (TP-Link router) while the Host is on 192.168.254.x. Routers block broadcast signals between subnets!

Recommended Fix: In your TP-Link Deco / Router app, switch Operation Mode to "Access Point (AP) Mode". All PCs will share the same network, allowing WoL magic packets to pass freely!
3 Windows 11 Fast Startup & Realtek Driver Fix

Windows 11 "Fast Startup" cuts power to network cards on shutdown. We created an automated 1-click batch fix on your Desktop:

Run on Gaming PC: ENABLE_WAKE_ON_LAN_FOR_GAMING_PC.bat
4 Motherboard BIOS Checklist
  • Power On By PCI-E / Wake on LAN: Set to ENABLED.
  • ErP / EuP Ready: Set to DISABLED (ErP shuts off Ethernet power completely).
100% Guaranteed Method from Anywhere: Smart Plug

If your PC was unplugged or router blocks WoL, plug your Gaming PC into a $8 Smart Plug (Tapo / Tuya). In your PC BIOS, set "Restore AC Power Loss" to Power On. Toggling the plug on your phone turns on your PC every single time!
